About Jack

Banking Is in Jack's Blood

From the Bank of NSW in the 1930s to Home Loan Essentials today. A family story built on trust and service spanning nearly a century.

Jack grew up in a banking family. His great-grandfather started at the Bank of NSW in the 1930s, his grandfather spent over 30 years at Westpac, and both his parents worked in financial services.

He started Home Loan Essentials to offer something that is harder to find than it should be: straight answers, no hard sell, and a broker who stays with you through the entire process.
