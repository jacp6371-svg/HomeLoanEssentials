Frequently Asked Questions

Home Loan Questions, Answered.

Everything you need to know about home loans, mortgage broking, and working with Jack.

QWhat does a mortgage broker do?
A

A mortgage broker helps you compare home loan options from a range of lenders and guides you through the application process. Instead of approaching multiple banks yourself, a mortgage broker can assess your financial situation, recommend suitable loan products, assist with paperwork, and help secure competitive interest rates and loan features that match your goals.

QIs it better to use a mortgage broker or go directly to a bank?
A

A mortgage broker provides access to multiple lenders and loan products, allowing you to compare a wider range of options. Going directly to a bank limits you to that bank's products. A broker can help you find a loan that suits your circumstances, potentially saving you time and money.

QHow much can I borrow for a home loan?
A

The amount you can borrow depends on factors such as your income, expenses, existing debts, credit history, deposit size, and the lender's lending criteria. A mortgage broker can assess your situation and provide an estimate of your borrowing capacity before you start house hunting.

QHow much deposit do I need to buy a home?
A

Many lenders prefer a deposit of at least 20% of the property's value to avoid Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI). However, some borrowers may be able to purchase a property with a smaller deposit, depending on their circumstances and the lender's requirements.

QWhat is pre-approval for a home loan?
A

Pre-approval is an indication from a lender that they may be willing to lend you a certain amount based on an initial assessment of your finances. It can give you confidence when searching for a property and show sellers that you are a serious buyer.

QCan I refinance my existing home loan?
A

Yes. Refinancing allows you to replace your current home loan with a new one, often to secure a lower interest rate, reduce repayments, access equity, consolidate debts, or obtain better loan features. A mortgage broker can compare refinancing options across multiple lenders.

QWhat documents do I need to apply for a home loan?
A

Most lenders require proof of identity, income documents such as payslips or tax returns, bank statements, details of existing debts, and information about your assets and liabilities. The exact requirements may vary depending on the lender and your employment situation.

QCan self-employed borrowers get a home loan?
A

Yes. Self-employed borrowers can often qualify for home loans, although lenders may require additional documentation such as business financial statements, tax returns, and evidence of ongoing income. A mortgage broker can help identify lenders with flexible policies for self-employed applicants.

QWhat is Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI)?
A

Lenders Mortgage Insurance is a one-off insurance premium that may apply when a borrower has less than a 20% deposit. LMI protects the lender, not the borrower, and the cost can vary depending on the loan amount and deposit size.

A

Eligible first home buyers in Queensland may be able to access government grants and stamp duty concessions, depending on their circumstances and the property they are purchasing.
