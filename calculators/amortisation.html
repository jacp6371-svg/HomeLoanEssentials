How it works

The assumptions

Every calculator simplifies. Here is exactly what this one assumes, so you know how far to trust the number.

  • Monthly repayments at a fixed rate for the whole term.
  • The extra repayment is added every month from the first month.
  • Fees and offset accounts are not included.

Common questions

Questions people ask

What is an amortisation schedule?

A table that shows each repayment split into interest and principal, and the balance remaining. Early on, most of each repayment is interest; later, most of it reduces the loan.

Are extra repayments worth it?

Usually. Every extra dollar reduces the balance that interest is charged on. Check whether your loan allows extra repayments without fees, especially on fixed rates.
