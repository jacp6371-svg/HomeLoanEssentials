Common questions

Questions people ask

When do I pay LMI?

Usually when your loan is more than 80% of the property's value. The premium is a one-off cost, typically added to the loan or paid at settlement.

How can I avoid LMI?

Save a 20% deposit, use the 5% Deposit Scheme if you are an eligible first home buyer, have a family member provide a guarantee, or check whether your profession qualifies for a waiver.

Is LMI refundable if I refinance?

Generally no, or only a small portion within a short time. If you refinance above 80% later, you may pay it again.
