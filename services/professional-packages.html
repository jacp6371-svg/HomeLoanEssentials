Professional Packages

Some lenders offer special terms to eligible professionals, such as reduced or waived Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) with a smaller deposit. That can save thousands and get you into a home sooner. We check whether you qualify and which lenders offer the best version.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Still unsure? See the full FAQ or ask Jack directly.

What is an LMI waiver?

Lenders Mortgage Insurance protects the lender, not you, and is normally charged when you borrow more than 80% of the property's value. An LMI waiver means the lender does not charge it, even with a smaller deposit.

Which professions are eligible?

It depends on the lender. Medical, legal and accounting professions are the most common. Jack will check your profession against current lender policies.

Can I use a professional package for an investment property?

Some lenders allow it, others only for owner-occupied homes. We will confirm before you apply.
