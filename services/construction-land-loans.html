Construction & Land Loans

Buying a block now and building later, building an investment, or taking on more of the build yourself all need lenders who are comfortable with the extra risk. We know which lenders will consider your project and what they will ask for.

Who it suits

Is this you?

If any of these sound familiar, this is the right place to start.

  • Buyers purchasing vacant land to build on later
  • Investors building a dwelling, duplex or dual occupancy
  • Owner-builders (lender options are limited)
  • Anyone whose build does not fit a standard house and land package

How it works

Step by step

  1. The land and the plan

    We look at the block, zoning, your building timeline and budget.

  2. Lender requirements

    Land loans often need bigger deposits, and owner-builder loans have strict rules. We set clear expectations.

  3. Approval

    We secure approval for the land, the construction, or both.

  4. Progress payments

    During the build we help release each progress payment as stages are completed.

Before we start

What Jack needs from you

Contract for the land, plans and building contract or quotes, council approvals where relevant, plus your income and savings details.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Still unsure? See the full FAQ or ask Jack directly.

Can I buy land now and build later?

Yes. Many lenders offer land loans, though some have time limits on when building must start and may need a larger deposit.

Can owner-builders get finance?

A small number of lenders will consider owner-builder loans, usually with a bigger deposit and detailed costings. It is harder, but not impossible.

Are there stamp duty concessions for vacant land in Queensland?

Queensland has a first home vacant land concession for eligible buyers building their first home. Check current rules with the Queensland Revenue Office.
