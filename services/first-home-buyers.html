Good to know

Government help for first home buyers

Australia-wide

Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme

Lets eligible first home buyers buy with as little as a 5% deposit without paying Lenders Mortgage Insurance, because the government guarantees part of the loan. Since 1 October 2025 there are no income caps and no limit on the number of places.

Property price caps apply. In Queensland they are $1,000,000 in the capital city and regional centres (including the Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast) and $700,000 in other areas.

Queensland

First Home Owner Grant

A $30,000 grant for eligible first home buyers who buy or build a new home in Queensland valued under $750,000 (including land). Established homes do not qualify.

Queensland

Stamp duty (transfer duty) concessions

New homes: eligible first home buyers pay no transfer duty on a new home or off-the-plan purchase, with no value cap, for contracts signed on or after 1 May 2025.

Established homes: no transfer duty on a first home valued at $700,000 or less, with a partial concession up to $800,000.

From 1 August 2026 these concessions are generally limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents and specified foreign retirees.

Scheme rules, caps and grant amounts change, and eligibility depends on your circumstances. Always confirm current eligibility with Housing Australia and the Queensland Revenue Office (or your state's revenue office) before you sign a contract. Jack will check this with you as part of your application.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Still unsure? See the full FAQ or ask Jack directly.

How much deposit do I need for my first home?

With the 5% Deposit Scheme, as little as 5% of the purchase price if you are eligible and the home is under the price cap. Without it, 20% avoids LMI, and many lenders accept 5% to 10% with LMI added. You will also need money for stamp duty (if it applies), legal fees and moving costs.

Can I get the First Home Owner Grant on an established home in Queensland?

No. The Queensland grant is for new homes only, valued under $750,000 including land. Buyers of established homes may still benefit from the first home stamp duty concession.

Can I use the grant and the 5% Deposit Scheme together?

In many cases, yes. They are separate programs with separate rules, so Jack will check you against each one.

Do I need pre-approval before I make an offer?

It is strongly recommended. It tells you your budget and shows agents you are serious. It is not a final approval, so we still need the lender to sign off on the property itself.

Does a broker cost first home buyers anything?

No. Our service is free to you because lenders pay us when your loan settles.
