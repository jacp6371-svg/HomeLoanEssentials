Who it suits

Is this you?

If any of these sound familiar, this is the right place to start.

  • Owner-occupiers buying their next home, upgrading or downsizing

How it works

Step by step

  1. A straight conversation

    We talk through your income, savings, the home you are after and how you like to manage money. No obligation and no cost to you.

  2. Borrowing power and budget

    Jack works out what lenders are likely to approve and, just as important, what repayment feels comfortable for you.

  3. Compare and choose

Common questions

Questions people ask

Still unsure? See the full FAQ or ask Jack directly.

How much deposit do I need to buy a home?

Most lenders want 20% of the purchase price to avoid Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI). Many will lend with 5% to 10% if you pay LMI, and some professionals can have it reduced or waived. Jack can show you what each option costs.

Should I sell first or buy first?

It depends on your equity, the market and your nerves. Selling first gives certainty on price; buying first secures the home you want but may need bridging finance. We map out both so you can see the numbers side by side.

Does using a broker cost me anything?

No. Lenders pay brokers a commission when a loan settles, so our service costs you nothing. We will always tell you how we are paid.

How long does pre-approval last?

Usually three to six months depending on the lender. If it is close to expiring, we can refresh it.
